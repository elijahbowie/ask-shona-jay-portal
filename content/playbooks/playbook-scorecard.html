How each lesson was held

The bar, and the gate that enforced it

A lesson could not pass on structure alone. The 100-point rubric weights accuracy at 20 points with a hard gate: a page scoring below 17/20 on verified tax specifics could not reach a passing total, no matter how well organized. Every figure, threshold, deadline, and form number was confirmed against a canonical source (IRS.gov, Treasury, the statute, or an official state DOR) by a reviewer independent of the author. Numbers that no public source could confirm were flagged [confirm with your advisor] rather than invented.

What the fact-check caught

Representative corrections the loop forced

1099 Income Flow. First draft asserted the 2026 QBI single-filer phase-in threshold as $201,775 (that is actually the married-filing-separately figure). The reviewer caught it against IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32 §4.26 and the revision corrected it to $201,750, added the SSTB full phase-out tops ($276,750 single / $553,500 MFJ), and replaced practitioner-blog citations with the canonical IRS revenue procedure.
Form 2553 timing. Draft language "75 days from Jan 1" was corrected to the statutory standard "no more than 2 months and 15 days after the beginning of the tax year" (IRC §1362(b)), keeping March 16, 2026 as the calendar deadline.
Accountable plan / S-corp health. A bare "$100-per-day excise tax" warning was softened to reflect IRS Notice 2015-17 relief for single 2%-shareholder arrangements, so the page does not overstate risk for the exact scenario it describes.
