# Implementation-Playbook Quality Rubric (100 points)

A lesson passes only when its **total score exceeds 90** AND no single category scores below 60% of its weight AND the Accuracy gate (cat. 5) is ≥ 17/20.

The governing standard: **a reader of average intelligence who is NOT a tax expert can use this wiki page plus its attached download(s) as a complete implementation playbook for the strategy, without needing any context, definition, number, or step from outside the wiki.** Every term is defined on the page. Every number is on the page. Every step is on the page.

| # | Category | Weight | What full marks looks like |
|---|----------|--------|----------------------------|
| 1 | **Self-containedness / no outside context** | 20 | A non-expert can execute end-to-end from this page + downloads alone. Every jargon term is defined inline on first use. No "ask your CPA what X means" gaps. No undefined acronyms. |
| 2 | **Applicability & non-fit** | 10 | Crisp "who this is for" and an explicit "who must NOT use this / when it backfires." A reader can self-qualify in under a minute. |
| 3 | **Step-by-step workflow** | 15 | Ordered, numbered, concrete actions. Each step says exactly what to do, with what input, producing what output. A reader never wonders "what do I actually do now?" |
| 4 | **Required facts, documents & records** | 10 | Exact list of inputs to gather first and records to keep after, with retention guidance. |
| 5 | **Accuracy & currency of tax specifics (GATE)** | 20 | Every figure, threshold, percentage, deadline, form number, and statute is correct for the current tax year and **verified against a canonical source** (IRS.gov, Treasury, official state DOR, statute). Sources cited. Nothing fabricated. Items that cannot be verified are marked `[confirm with your advisor]`, never guessed. |
| 6 | **Timing, deadlines & cadence** | 5 | Concrete dates/windows and the recurring rhythm (monthly/quarterly/annual) the strategy requires. |
| 7 | **Risk, caveats, mistakes & review gates** | 10 | Entity/payroll/state/federal/current-year caveats; the most common mistakes; explicit advisor-review triggers and a before-you-act gate. |
| 8 | **Downloads integration** | 5 | The attached worksheet/template/checklist is named, and the page explains exactly where in the workflow to use it and how to fill it. |
| 9 | **Clarity, structure & brand voice** | 5 | Plain language (~8th grade), scannable headings, no marketing fluff, no em dashes, "your advisor" framing (never personal names), required disclaimer present. |

**Scoring discipline:** be honest and adversarial. A 90+ means genuinely ready. Most first drafts of technical tax content land 70–85; the gap is almost always (a) undefined jargon, (b) a number that is asserted but not verified, or (c) a step that assumes outside knowledge.
